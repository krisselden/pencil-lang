A term is what's between spaces

Two expressions with the same two states, on one clock. The second state writes b at another place. They differ only in their spaces.

Written together

on write: "2ab" at product
on part: "2a" at product, "b" at apart

One term, 2ab, and the next state has none, so it fades out and the rest fades in. Nothing moves.

With spaces

on write: "2 a b" at product
on part: "2 a" at product, "b" at apart

Three terms. The next state has all three, with b somewhere else, so b alone moves there.
