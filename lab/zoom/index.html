Keeping a reader oriented when the view moves

Both figures from the Doyle series, with the options running side by side from one set of controls. Each is a port of the site's figure, and the left or first panel is what the site does now. In both, the camera's path is the same. What differs is what moves with it, and when.

Why only three neighbors? Slider drags

The circles and the view follow different clocks on the site: the circles change on each step, and the view eases after them. Sweep at 25 ms a step is the speed where you saw it vibrate.

What's the tangent line in two dimensions? All the way in and out

The camera's path is its own inverse in all of them. On the site, the subject changes as soon as a move starts, so coming out it's gone until the view reaches it. Holding it means showing the shallowest requested step that's in frame, going either way. Watch the 3D views.
